Google Voice, as a real desktop app.

GVoicePlus keeps Google Voice in its own window with a tray icon, unread badge and notifications you can reply to. Then it lets you change how it looks and what shows.

Download for Mac or Windows View on GitHub

Free, with the source on GitHub. Unofficial, not made by Google.

Messages and contacts are blurred for privacy.

Features

Everything Google Voice is missing on the desktop

You still sign in with your own Google account and use the normal Google Voice website. GVoicePlus adds the parts around it.

Themes

Material-style colors in Dark and Light, plus classic themes like Dracula, Solar, Minty, Cerulean and DarkPlus.

App icons

13 colors of one speech-bubble icon, or use your own image (PNG, JPG or .icns).

Zoom

Set a zoom level in Settings, or use the usual keys (Cmd or Ctrl with +, - and 0). The box follows the keys.

Your top bar

Click a picture of Google Voice's top bar to hide or show its items: menu, search, status, Upgrade, help, apps and more.

Sidebars on your terms

Hide the dialer sidebar, or collapse the main sidebar and have it reappear when you hover.

Reply from notifications

Answer a text straight from its notification without switching windows. Works on Mac and Windows.

Verification codes

Mac only. Copies a 4 to 8 digit code from a text to your clipboard. For codes of 6 or more digits it also marks the thread read. Off until you turn it on.

Images

Paste or drag and drop images into a message.

Stays out of the way

Start at logon, start minimized to the tray, exit on close if you want, and spell checking.

Appearance

Make it look like yours

Pick a theme, flip between Dark and Light, and choose the icon that sits in your dock or taskbar.

  • Material-style and classic themes, each with its own accent color
  • 13 app icon colors, or your own image
  • A zoom box that matches the keyboard shortcuts

Layout

Show only what you use

Google Voice has a lot of chrome. Click an item in the top bar picture to hide it, and click again to bring it back. Show all resets everything.

  • Hide the menu, search, status, Upgrade, help, apps and account
  • Hide the dialer sidebar
  • Collapse the main sidebar, and optionally show it again on hover

General

Set it up once

A short list of options covers how the app starts, how it closes and what it does with the texts you receive.

  • Start at logon, start minimized to the tray, exit when the window closes
  • Spell check (needs a restart)
  • Copy verification codes (Mac only)
  • Tell me about updates, which only notifies and never installs

Light and dark

Match your desktop

The Material-style themes come in both Dark and Light.

First launch

The apps are not code signed, so macOS and Windows both show a warning the first time.

  1. Mac: drag GVoicePlus into Applications. Then run this once in Terminal and open the app. Or use System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GVoicePlus.app"
  2. Windows: run the installer. When SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

Privacy

Your account stays yours

Your sign-in and settings are stored on your computer. Besides Google Voice itself, the only network request GVoicePlus adds is the optional update check, which asks GitHub for the latest release of this project. It tells you when a new version exists and never installs anything.
